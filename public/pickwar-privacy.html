PickWar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

PickWar is a free Android app published by Orangiraffe LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: PickWar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. PickWar has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Information stored on your device

The projects and options you create in PickWar (names, text, scores, and rankings) are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Photos and files

If you choose to add an image to an option, PickWar opens your device's photo picker or file picker so you can select it. The app only uses the images you select, and only to show them inside the app. Selected images are not uploaded or sent anywhere. PickWar does not use your camera or microphone.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we do not collect it. PickWar does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children

PickWar does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form.
