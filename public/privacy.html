Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy covers the website at orangiraffe.com, operated by Orangiraffe LLC ("we", "us"). It does not cover our apps.

Looking for an app's privacy policy?

Each app has its own policy. For Dromotelo, on the web and in its mobile and watch apps, see the Dromotelo Privacy Policy.

What this website collects

Only what you choose to send us through the contact form. This website has:

  • no user accounts or sign-in,
  • no cookies, and no local storage,
  • no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind,
  • no third-party scripts, fonts, embeds or other outside resources.

We do not keep access logs for this website. To deliver a page, our server necessarily sees your IP address and the request your browser sends. If the server software hits an error, it may record a short technical message, which can include an IP address; those messages are kept only in a small, size-limited log that is overwritten automatically.

The contact form

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter. They are saved on our own server, in a private inbox that only we can open, and are not sent to any other company. We use them only to reply to you, and we do not sell them or share them for marketing. We keep a message until we delete it.

We do not store your IP address with your message. To stop abuse, the form counts submissions per IP address for up to an hour; that count is held in memory only and is never written to disk.

Hosting

The website runs on a server we operate, rented from a hosting provider in the United States. The provider stores the server but does not use the data on it for its own purposes.

Children

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Orangiraffe LLC, a Texas limited liability company. For privacy questions, use our contact form.
