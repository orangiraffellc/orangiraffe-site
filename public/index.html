Dromotelo

Turns a runner's goal time into a personalized training plan with exact paces, for the 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon. Syncs with Garmin, Strava, COROS and Polar.

Available on the web. An Android phone app and a Wear OS watch app are in testing.

Visit dromotelo.com

PickWar

Settle any choice, one battle at a time. Add your options as text or photos, compare two at a time, and get a ranked winner. Use it to choose a logo for your business, pick baby names, name a new pet, decide on a vacation spot, choose a paint color, find the best photo for your profile, or settle where to eat tonight. Free, with no sign-up, no ads, and no tracking.

Android app coming soon to Google Play. Your projects and photos stay on your device.
